Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

OmShare is designed with privacy as the core principle. We don't store files, don't track users, and don't collect personal data. Your transfers are completely private and secure.

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We don't store file contents
  • ❌ We don't track individual users
  • ❌ We don't collect personal information
  • ❌ We don't use cookies for tracking
  • ❌ We don't share data with third parties
  • ❌ We don't maintain logs of file transfers

What Happens to Your Data

Transfer Codes: We store 6-digit codes temporarily in our database to enable peer matching. These expire automatically after 24 hours.

Signaling Data: WebRTC signaling information (offers, answers, ICE candidates) is stored briefly during active transfers and deleted upon completion.

File Content: Never stored. Your files transfer directly between browsers using P2P connections.

Cookies & Local Storage

OmShare may use browser localStorage to store your preferences (like dark mode setting). No tracking cookies are used. You can clear this data anytime in your browser settings.

Security

All data in transit is encrypted using:

  • WebRTC DTLS encryption (data channels)
  • HTTPS for all connections
  • Firebase Firestore security rules

Third-Party Services

OmShare uses Firebase for signaling infrastructure. Firebase may collect basic analytics (non-identifying). See Firebase's privacy policy for details: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Data Retention

  • Transfer codes: 24 hours
  • Signaling data: Duration of transfer only
  • Rate limit records: 24 hours
  • File content: Never stored

GDPR & Data Rights

Since OmShare doesn't collect personal data, GDPR rights are minimally applicable. You have the right to request deletion of any stored signaling data. Contact: privacy@omshare.app

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at: privacy@omshare.app

Policy Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here with the updated date.
